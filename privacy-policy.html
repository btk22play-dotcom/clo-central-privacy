Privacy Policy

CLO Central · Last updated: September 25, 2026

CLO Central ("the app") is a private, members-only app for residents of the Cooperative Living Organization (CLO), a student housing cooperative in Gainesville, Florida. The app is operated by Ben Kacso on behalf of CLO ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

CLO Central is intended only for CLO residents aged 18 or older. Accounts must be approved by a CLO executive before they can be used. The app is not directed to, and not intended for use by, anyone under 18.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the app, we collect information you provide and information generated by your use of the app, including:

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and room number.
  • Profile information (optional): a profile photo, dietary restrictions or allergies, and an emergency contact.
  • Community activity: chore ("detail") completion records, chore swap requests, sign-ups for work days, parking, or flyering, and event participation.
  • Fines and bonuses: records of fines or bonuses issued to you, including amounts, reasons, and any notes or photos attached by the fine committee, and any related information you submit.
  • Messages: messages you post in the app's group chats.
  • Role and status: your member role(s) and account approval status.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to operate the app and support the running of the cooperative, including to:

  • Create and manage your account and verify you are a CLO resident;
  • Show you your chores, schedules, sign-ups, fines, bonuses, and community events;
  • Enable community features such as group chat and chore swaps;
  • Let managers and the fine committee carry out their responsibilities;
  • Send you in-app notifications about activity relevant to you.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who Can See Your Information

CLO Central is a shared community tool, so some information is visible to other approved members by design:

  • Your name and profile photo are visible to other members (for example, next to your chat messages and in member lists).
  • Messages you post in a group chat are visible to the members of that chat.
  • Chore schedules, swap postings, and sign-up lists are visible to members.
  • Your fines and bonuses are visible to you and to CLO executives and the fine committee; they are not shown to other members.
  • CLO executives, board members, managers, and the fine committee can see additional information as needed to perform their roles.

Your information is not shared with the general public or with people outside the approved CLO membership through the app.

How Your Information Is Stored

The app's data and uploaded files are stored using Supabase, a third-party hosting and database provider, which processes and stores this information on our behalf. Access is protected by authentication and role-based security rules. Some preferences (such as your app-lock PIN) are stored only on your own device and are never sent to our servers.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to run the cooperative. Some community records (such as fine and bonus history, or messages retained for community integrity) may be kept after they are created for recordkeeping. You can delete your account at any time (see below).

Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and update: you can view and update your profile information in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account from within the app, in the account settings. Deleting your account removes your profile; some shared or community records may be retained or anonymized as described above.
  • Questions or requests: you can contact us using the email below for any privacy question or request.

Children's Privacy

CLO Central is intended only for CLO residents aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
